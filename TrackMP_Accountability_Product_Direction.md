# TrackMP — Product Direction & Accountability Niches

> **Vision:** Build a unique civic accountability platform that bridges the gap between raw government data and everyday citizen understanding.

## The Core Opportunity

To build a unique platform in this space, TrackMP should focus on **diagonal accountability** — connecting government data, political activity, public institutions, and real-world citizen experience in a way that is understandable and actionable.

Instead of becoming another database of political information, the platform can differentiate itself by **turning complex public data into meaningful accountability signals**.

This document outlines four high-utility domain niches that can form the foundation for the platform.

---

## 1. 🎮 The "Gamified Citizen" Niche

### The Angle

Make tracking politics engaging by turning civic participation and political accountability into an interactive experience.

### Key Features

#### 🏆 Constituency Leaderboards

Dynamically rank local MLAs and MPs using verified metrics such as:

- Attendance
- Questions asked
- Parliamentary participation
- Bills and debates participated in
- Fund utilization
- Constituency-related activity

Leaderboards can be filtered by:

- Constituency
- State
- Political party
- Election
- Time period
- MP vs. MLA

#### 📊 Civic Scorecards

Give politicians a simple, easy-to-understand **FICO-like civic score**, for example:

> **Civic Score: 8.4 / 10**

The score should be derived from transparent, verifiable data rather than subjective opinions.

Each score should explain:

- What contributed to the score
- Which metrics were used
- What period was analyzed
- Where the underlying data came from

#### 🔔 Follow & Alert System

Allow citizens to follow their local representatives and receive notifications when:

- Their representative speaks in Parliament
- Their representative misses a session
- A new question is submitted
- A bill is introduced or voted on
- Important constituency activity is recorded

Potential notification channels:

- Web push
- Email
- WhatsApp
- Telegram

### Why This Is Different

Many existing political-information websites feel like complex spreadsheets.

This approach makes civic information:

- Visual
- Simple
- Interactive
- Shareable
- Accessible to Gen Z and millennial voters

### Product Principle

> **Don't make citizens study political data. Make political accountability easy to understand.**

## 2. 🤖 The AI-Powered "Deep Scrutiny" Niche

### The Angle

Use AI to cross-reference **what politicians say** against **what they actually do**.

The goal is not to automatically label politicians as dishonest. Instead, the platform should surface **verifiable inconsistencies, changes, and evidence** and allow users to examine the underlying sources.

### Key Features

#### 🗣️ Speech vs. Vote Tracker

Use AI/LLMs to analyze:

- Parliamentary speeches
- Legislative transcripts
- Public statements
- Manifestos
- Interviews
- Social media statements

Then compare those statements against:

- Parliamentary votes
- Legislative actions
- Committee participation
- Government records

The system could flag potential contradictions.

**Example:**

> A representative publicly supports a policy but later votes against legislation related to that policy.

The platform should show:

1. The original statement
2. The relevant vote
3. The dates
4. The source documents
5. The AI-generated comparison
6. A human-readable explanation

#### 🧾 AI Manifestometer

Allow users to paste a:

- Campaign speech
- Election manifesto
- Public promise
- Policy proposal

The AI can then cross-reference those claims with:

- Government budgets
- Official expenditure data
- Economic statistics
- Existing legislation
- Government policy documents

The system can estimate whether promises appear financially or administratively feasible.

> **Important:** AI output should be presented as analysis or an evidence-based assessment, not as an unquestionable fact.

#### 📈 Affidavit Anomaly Detection

Compare politicians' self-declared election affidavits over multiple election cycles.

Potentially highlight:

- Significant asset changes
- Liability changes
- Changes in declared income
- Property changes
- Company/business interests
- Other unusual patterns

For example:

> **Asset change detected:** Declared assets increased significantly between election cycles.

The platform should provide the underlying affidavits and calculations so users can independently verify the result.

### Why This Is Different

Existing platforms often **host political data**.

TrackMP can go one step further:

> **Collect → Compare → Analyze → Explain → Cite the evidence**

The key differentiator is not merely AI. It is **AI-assisted evidence analysis with transparent source attribution**.

### Product Principle

> **AI should help citizens discover questions — not manufacture accusations.**

## 3. 📍 Hyper-Local "Diagonal" Accountability Niche

### The Angle

Shift accountability beyond major national politicians and focus on the institutions and representatives closest to everyday citizens.

Potential targets include:

- Municipal corporation councillors
- Municipal wards
- Gram Panchayats
- Local government bodies
- Ward committees
- Local development projects

### Key Features

#### 💰 Hyper-Local Budgets

Show citizens exactly how public money is allocated to local areas.

Examples:

- Pothole repairs
- Streetlights
- Water pipelines
- Drainage
- Roads
- Schools
- Public facilities

Where possible, connect:

**Budget → Project → Representative → Location → Status**

#### 📍 Geotagged Proof

Allow citizens to upload photos of unfinished, damaged, or abandoned projects.

Examples:

- Incomplete roads
- Broken streetlights
- Damaged schools
- Unfinished drainage work
- Water infrastructure problems

Each report could be associated with:

- Location
- Project
- Ward
- Local representative
- Government department
- Date
- Photos
- Status

This creates a bridge between **official government records and real-world conditions**.

#### 🏛️ Ward Committee Trackers

Track and publish:

- Meeting calendars
- Meeting dates
- Minutes
- Decisions
- Attendance
- Follow-up actions

This can expose activity that is technically public but often difficult for ordinary citizens to find.

### Why This Is Different

Most political accountability platforms focus heavily on:

- MPs
- National politics
- Elections
- Major political parties

But citizens experience government primarily at the local level.

The biggest accountability gap may exist where citizens interact with government every day:

> **Roads → Water → Drainage → Waste → Schools → Local infrastructure**

### Product Principle

> **Accountability should exist where citizens actually experience government.**

## 4. 🏛️ The "Horizontal Watchdog" Tracker

### The Angle

Don't only track politicians.

Track how effectively **state institutions themselves check political power**.

This creates a broader accountability layer across the political and administrative system.

### Key Features

#### 📄 RTI Response Index

Track how government departments and public authorities respond to Right to Information (RTI) requests.

Potential metrics:

- Response rate
- Response time
- Rejection rate
- Delay rate
- Appeal rate
- Information completeness

This could eventually produce an institutional metric such as:

> **RTI Responsiveness: 72 / 100**

The methodology should be transparent and based on documented records.

#### 🏛️ Committee Attendance Logs

Parliamentary Standing Committees and similar legislative committees perform significant legislative scrutiny.

Track:

- Committee membership
- Attendance
- Meetings
- Participation where publicly documented
- Reports
- Recommendations

This provides accountability beyond the headline-level parliamentary attendance figures.

#### ⚖️ Judicial Delay Tracker

Monitor publicly available information about criminal cases pending against sitting lawmakers.

Potential metrics:

- Case status
- Court
- Filing date
- Hearing history
- Current stage
- Adjournments where available
- Case duration

The platform must distinguish very clearly between:

> **An allegation or pending case ≠ a conviction or finding of guilt.**

### Why This Is Different

This model holds multiple parts of the system accountable:

```text
                 CITIZENS
                    │
                    ▼
             ┌──────────────┐
             │   POLITICS   │
             └──────┬───────┘
                    │
        ┌───────────┼───────────┐
        ▼           ▼           ▼
   Legislature   Executive   Institutions
        │           │           │
        └───────────┼───────────┘
                    ▼
             PUBLIC ACCOUNTABILITY
```

Instead of asking only:

> "Is this politician performing?"

The platform can also ask:

> "Are the institutions responsible for checking power functioning effectively?"

### Product Principle

> **A healthy democracy requires accountability between institutions, not just accountability of individuals.**

Use deterministic calculations and AI-assisted analysis to identify:

# Critical Trust & Accuracy Principles

Because the platform deals with politicians, criminal cases, public allegations, AI analysis, and potentially sensitive political information, credibility should be treated as a core product feature.

## 1. Separate facts from analysis

Clearly distinguish:

- **Verified fact**
- **Calculated metric**
- **AI-generated analysis**
- **Citizen-submitted report**
- **Unverified claim**

## 2. Always provide sources

Important claims should have a source trail wherever possible.

## 3. Avoid automated accusations

AI should identify:

> "Potential inconsistency detected"

rather than making unsupported claims such as:

> "Politician X is lying."

## 4. Preserve context

A vote, speech, asset increase, court case, or absence should be presented with sufficient context to avoid misleading conclusions.

## 5. Make scoring transparent

If a politician receives a score:

```text
Civic Score: 8.2 / 10

Attendance       9.1
Questions        8.4
Participation    7.8
Fund Utilization 8.6
────────────────────
Overall          8.2
```

Users should be able to understand **exactly how the score was calculated**.

## 6. Keep AI explainable

AI-generated insights should provide:

- Evidence
- Sources
- Relevant dates
- Reasoning summary
- Confidence/uncertainty where appropriate

# North Star

## Make Government Accountability Understandable

TrackMP should aim to answer the questions ordinary citizens actually have:

- **Who represents me?**
- **What have they actually done?**
- **How often do they participate?**
- **What did they promise?**
- **What did they vote for?**
- **How are public funds being used?**
- **What is happening in my local area?**
- **Are government institutions responding?**
- **Are there meaningful changes or anomalies in the available evidence?**
- **Where can I verify the information myself?**

The ultimate goal is not simply to publish more political data.

It is to make **public accountability easier to discover, understand, verify, and act upon.**
