2 Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to information processed through the TrackMP website, public pages, contact forms, report and correction forms, contributor access requests, and approved dashboard or administrative access. It does not govern third-party websites or services linked from TrackMP.

3 Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Provide

Depending on the interaction, you may provide your name or display name, email/contact details, messages, reports, corrections, contributor-access information, account/authentication information, and other information you voluntarily submit.

🔒 Please avoid unnecessary personal information. Do not include sensitive personal information about yourself or another person in a report or correction unless it is genuinely necessary for us to evaluate the submission.

3.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit TrackMP, technical and usage information may be collected automatically through our website infrastructure and analytics services, including browser/device characteristics, screen resolution, approximate geographic information, pages viewed, referring pages, interaction events, and technical information needed for security and reliability.

3.3 Public and Third-Party Information

TrackMP may collect, organize, analyze, and present information from publicly available and third-party sources concerning politicians, elections, political parties, public records, statements, commitments, financial disclosures, and related subjects.

Information displayed on TrackMP may relate to identifiable individuals. Public availability does not mean every item is necessarily complete, current, or independently verified. Our sourcing and correction practices are described in the Data & Editorial Methodology.

4 How We Use Information

  • Operate, maintain, and secure TrackMP;
  • Provide public information and research functionality;
  • Respond to questions and communications;
  • Review reports, corrections, and source-related submissions;
  • Evaluate and administer contributor/dashboard access;
  • Detect spam, fraud, abuse, malicious activity, and automated attacks;
  • Understand website usage and improve content, navigation, and performance;
  • Diagnose technical problems and monitor reliability;
  • Maintain records and comply with applicable legal obligations; and
  • Exercise or defend legal rights where reasonably necessary.

5 Google Analytics 4

TrackMP uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand website usage and improve the platform. Our web stream uses Google tag Measurement ID G-18EZD9W5GG.

Enhanced Measurement is currently enabled for page views, scrolls, outbound clicks, site search, supported video engagement, file downloads, and form interactions.

Analytics may process browser/device characteristics, approximate location information, page URLs and titles, referrer information, interaction events, and pseudonymous identifiers used to distinguish browser or device activity. TrackMP does not intentionally configure GA4 to receive the contents of contact, report, correction, or contributor-request forms.

Google Signals is currently disabled in TrackMP's GA4 configuration.

For Google's practices, see Google Privacy Policy and Google Analytics Help.

6 Analytics Retention

TrackMP's current GA4 configuration uses a 2-month event-data retention period and a 14-month user-data retention period for the applicable GA4 data covered by those settings. "Reset on new user activity" is currently enabled.

These settings apply to relevant user-level and event-level Analytics data and should not be interpreted as meaning that every form of aggregated Analytics reporting disappears after the same period.

7 Cookies and Similar Technologies

TrackMP and third-party services may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies to operate the website, provide security, measure usage, and support technical preferences. Google Analytics may use analytics identifiers and related technologies to measure interactions.

Your browser may allow you to block or delete cookies. Blocking certain technologies may affect functionality or analytics measurement.

8 Google reCAPTCHA v3

TrackMP uses Google reCAPTCHA v3 to help protect forms and relevant functionality against spam, abuse, automated submissions, and malicious activity.

reCAPTCHA may process technical and interaction information needed to assess whether activity is likely to be automated or abusive. This processing is performed by Google and is subject to Google's privacy practices.

See Google Privacy Policy and Google Terms of Service.

9 Contact, Report, and Correction Forms

Information submitted through TrackMP forms may be reviewed by TrackMP personnel or authorized contributors to evaluate the request, verify information, communicate with the submitter, and improve or correct TrackMP records.

Submitting a correction or report does not guarantee that TrackMP will change or remove information. We may consider reliability, relevance, source quality, and supporting evidence before making a change.

10 Contributor and Administrative Access

Ordinary visitors do not need an account. TrackMP may provide approved contributors or administrators with dashboard access after a request and review.

For this purpose, we may process account identifiers, contact information, authentication-related data, permissions, and security/activity logs. Access may be suspended or revoked when reasonably necessary to protect TrackMP, users, data, or record integrity.

11 Information We Do Not Intentionally Send to GA4

TrackMP does not intentionally configure Google Analytics to collect message contents from contact, report, correction, or contributor-access forms. We also do not intentionally send passwords or authentication credentials to GA4.

Page URLs and titles are measured as part of normal analytics. We take reasonable steps to avoid placing unnecessary personal information in URLs, query parameters, page titles, or custom analytics parameters.

12 Sharing and Disclosure

We may provide access to information to service providers reasonably necessary to operate TrackMP, including hosting, infrastructure, security, analytics, communications, database, and anti-abuse providers.

We may also disclose information when required by law or lawful process; to protect rights, safety, security, or property; to investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents; in connection with a corporate transaction where permitted; or with your direction or authorization.

13 International Processing

TrackMP is operated from India, but some service providers may process information in India or other countries. Where applicable, information may therefore be processed outside your country. We take reasonable steps to use providers and arrangements appropriate to the information and applicable legal requirements.

14 Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to maintain records, resolve disputes, protect the service, comply with legal obligations, or otherwise as permitted by applicable law.

Retention varies by information type. A correction request, for example, may need to be retained to maintain an audit trail. Google Analytics retention is governed separately by the GA4 settings described above.

15 Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, misuse, or destruction. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

16 Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal data, including access, correction, updating, deletion, withdrawal of consent where applicable, grievance handling, or other rights provided by law.

To make a privacy request, contact us using the details below. We may need sufficient information to verify the request. Available rights and remedies vary by applicable law.

This policy is not a legal opinion and does not represent that every provision of Indian or foreign privacy law applies to TrackMP. Applicability depends on the facts, jurisdiction, and status of the operator and service.
