Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how TrackMP uses cookies and similar technologies to operate, secure, measure, and improve the website.

📋 Draft for implementation and legal review. Replace bracketed placeholders before publication. This policy reflects TrackMP's currently identified analytics and security technologies and should be updated if additional cookies, tags, advertising tools, or third-party services are introduced.
📅 Last updated: 6 August 2026

1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files or similar identifiers that websites may store on or access from your browser or device. They can help a website remember information, maintain functionality, measure usage, and support security.

TrackMP and third-party services used by TrackMP may also use technologies that perform functions similar to cookies, including browser storage, tags, pixels, or identifiers.

2 How TrackMP Uses Cookies and Similar Technologies

TrackMP uses these technologies for limited purposes including:

  • Operating and securing website functionality;
  • Preventing spam, abuse, and automated attacks;
  • Understanding how visitors use TrackMP;
  • Measuring page views and website interactions;
  • Improving website content, navigation, and performance; and
  • Supporting technical diagnostics and reliability.

TrackMP does not currently use cookies for a dedicated advertising or behavioral-advertising program.

4 Google Analytics 4

TrackMP uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors use the website and to improve the platform. The current web stream uses Google tag Measurement ID G-18EZD9W5GG.

GA4 may use cookies, browser storage, and other identifiers to distinguish browser or device activity and associate related interactions for analytics purposes.

Depending on Google's processing, analytics information may include page URLs and titles, referring information, browser and device characteristics, screen resolution, language settings, approximate geographic information, interaction events, and pseudonymous identifiers.

Enhanced Measurement

TrackMP currently has GA4 Enhanced Measurement enabled. Depending on the relevant feature and configuration, this can measure:

  • Page views;
  • Scroll activity;
  • Outbound link clicks;
  • Site search activity;
  • Video engagement for supported embedded videos;
  • File downloads; and
  • Form interactions.

Google Signals

Google Signals data collection is currently disabled for TrackMP's GA4 property. TrackMP therefore does not intentionally enable Google Signals as part of its current analytics configuration.

Analytics Retention

TrackMP currently has GA4 event-data retention configured for 2 months and user-data retention configured for 14 months. The "reset on new user activity" option is currently enabled.

These settings concern the applicable user-level and event-level Analytics data. They should not be interpreted as meaning that all aggregated reporting or every form of data processed by Google is deleted after the same period.

5 Google reCAPTCHA v3

TrackMP uses Google reCAPTCHA v3 to help protect forms and relevant functionality against spam, abuse, automated submissions, and malicious activity.

reCAPTCHA may process technical and interaction information necessary to assess whether activity is likely to be automated or abusive. This processing is performed by Google and is subject to Google's own terms and privacy practices.

For more information, review Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Terms of Service.

6 Form Submissions and Analytics

TrackMP provides contact, report, correction, and contributor-access forms. TrackMP does not intentionally configure GA4 to send the contents of these form submissions to Google Analytics.

Analytics may measure that a form interaction or submission occurred where Enhanced Measurement or another configured event records that interaction. The contents of the submission should remain separate from analytics event data.

🔒 Implementation note: TrackMP should avoid placing names, email addresses, messages, report details, passwords, tokens, or other unnecessary personal information into URLs, page titles, GA4 event parameters, or other analytics fields.

7 Necessary Website Technologies

Some website functionality may require browser storage, cookies, session identifiers, security tokens, or similar mechanisms. These technologies may be necessary for authentication, contributor/admin dashboard access, security, request handling, rate limiting, or other essential functions.

Where such technologies are used, they should be limited to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant function.

8 Third-Party Technologies

TrackMP currently uses Google services for analytics and reCAPTCHA. Google may process information on its own systems and may use cookies or similar technologies according to its own policies.

TrackMP may add additional third-party services in the future. If a new service materially changes how cookies or similar technologies are used, this Cookie Policy may be updated.

9 How to Control Cookies

Most modern browsers allow you to view, block, delete, or restrict cookies through browser settings. The available controls differ by browser and device.

You can generally use your browser's privacy or site-settings controls to manage cookies and stored website data.

Blocking or deleting cookies may affect some website functions or prevent analytics from accurately measuring your visit.

10 Google Analytics Opt-Out Options

You may limit certain analytics collection through browser settings, privacy controls, or available Google tools. Google also provides information about its available controls and choices through its support resources.

See Google Analytics Help for current information about Analytics controls and configuration.

11 Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers provide a "Do Not Track" or similar privacy signal. Because browser implementations and standards vary, TrackMP does not currently represent that every such signal will automatically disable all analytics or third-party technologies.

Where legally required, TrackMP will implement applicable privacy-signal requirements.

12 Privacy and Personal Information

Cookies and similar technologies may involve information that can be associated with a browser, device, or online activity. TrackMP's broader handling of personal information is described in the Privacy Policy.

This Cookie Policy should therefore be read together with the Privacy Policy and, where applicable, the Terms of Use.

13 Changes to This Cookie Policy

TrackMP may update this Cookie Policy when its technology, analytics configuration, security systems, third-party services, or legal requirements change.

The "Last updated" date identifies the most recent revision. We recommend reviewing this page periodically.
